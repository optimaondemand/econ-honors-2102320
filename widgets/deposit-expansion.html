How Lending Creates Money

You deposit $1,000. The bank keeps a fraction as reserves and lends out the rest. The borrower spends it, and the seller deposits it in another bank. Press the button and follow the money round by round.

Textbook story, not today's rule. Since March 26, 2020, the Fed's required reserve ratio has been zero percent (Lesson 8.02). This lab shows how lending creates deposits when a bank keeps a fraction back. You choose the fraction.
