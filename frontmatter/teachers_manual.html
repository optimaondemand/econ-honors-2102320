Teacher's Manual

Economics Honors (Florida 2102320) · Teacher-facing only, hidden from students · companion to the Standards Alignment Matrix
What this manual is for. A quick-orientation guide for any teacher picking up this eight-module course — what each lesson teaches, which standard(s) it satisfies, what's confirmed-complete versus deferred, and a comprehensive standard-by-standard index so you can find every lesson and assessment that touches a given benchmark. This is a rebuild/rehost of an existing Canvas sandbox course into Optima's current delivery model; content decisions and their rationale are tracked in this build's plan.md decision log — this manual summarizes, it does not replace that log.
Read this before you touch the gradebook.
  • Modules 1–5 lesson assignments are full Tier-3-shaped items (written response, individually rubric-graded) — a deliberate, confirmed override of optima-canvas-assignments' usual Tier-3 ratio cap, matching the source course exactly. This is expected, not a defect. See plan.md decision-log item 7.
  • Modules 6–8 mix completion-scored and rubric-graded assignments to ease grading load. Ten of the fourteen lesson assignments are completion-scored (10 points for complete, 5 for partly complete, 0 for missing or off-topic; spot-check a handful for good faith, do not grade every one). Four are rubric-graded: 6.06, 7.04, 8.03, and 8.04. The three Module Projects are rubric-graded.
  • No due, lock, or unlock dates are set anywhere. Set real dates before running the course. The Suggested Schedule below is a reference only and never appears to students.
  • Exams. The Quarter 1 Exam (Modules 1–4) is the last item in Module 4. The Quarter 2 Exam (Modules 5–8 only) and the Final Exam (all eight modules) are the last two items in Module 8. Each is 40 multiple-choice questions, 40 points, with answers shuffled. They sit in their own Exams group (20% of the grade). The answer keys are in the exam specifications in the build folder.
  • Module 5's quizzes now have real questions (the source's empty shells were filled in a later remediation pass).
  • VR Lesson Resources pages exist for Lessons 1.01–5.05 (24 pages, platform-agnostic). Modules 6–8 do not have VR pages yet.
  • Primary sources. Five primary-source exercises (4 questions, 10 points each) appear in Modules 6–8: Bennett and Chapline (6.04), Roosevelt's First Inaugural (6.05), the Communist Manifesto (7.01), the FOMC statement of March 16, 2022 (8.02), and the CFPB Loan Estimate (8.03). The passages are typeset quotations; scans were not located. Two planned sources (Irving Fisher, 1929; Hayek, 1945) were dropped.
  • Virtue threads were proposed per module in CLAUDE.md but were never adopted. Treat them as inactive.
  • Grading weights are already configured: Reading Quizzes 25%, Writing Assignments 25%, Module Tests 20%, Exams 20%, Module Projects 10%.
